Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 8, 2026

1. Introduction

StoryForge Shorts ("we", "us", "our") is an automated video publishing service. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our website and our use of third-party platform APIs, including the TikTok API.

2. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information from visitors or viewers. Our website does not use cookies, analytics trackers, sign-up forms, or user accounts. We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or any other personally identifiable information from the public.

3. TikTok API Usage

We use the TikTok API solely to publish video content to our own TikTok account. Specifically:

  • We use the Content Posting API to upload videos to our TikTok account.
  • We do not access, collect, store, or process any TikTok user data.
  • We do not use TikTok Login or request access to any TikTok user's account.
  • We do not collect or share data from TikTok users, viewers, or followers.
  • Our TikTok API access tokens are stored securely and used exclusively for publishing content from our own account.

Our use of the TikTok API complies with TikTok's API Terms of Use and TikTok's Privacy Policy.

4. YouTube, Instagram & Facebook

We also publish content to YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook using their respective APIs. As with TikTok, we use these APIs solely to publish our own content and do not access or collect any user data through these integrations.

5. Content Sources

Our videos are based on publicly available stories from Reddit and similar public forums. We do not scrape private or protected content. Usernames from original posts may appear in video credits but are already publicly visible on the source platform.

6. Third-Party Services

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect basic server logs (IP addresses, browser type) as part of their hosting service. This is governed by GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Data Retention

Since we do not collect personal data, there is no user data to retain or delete. Video content published to social media platforms is subject to the retention policies of each respective platform.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data with third parties because we do not collect personal data.

10. Your Rights

If you believe any of our video content contains your personal information and you would like it removed, please contact us and we will review your request promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
