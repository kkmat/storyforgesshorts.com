Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

StoryForge Shorts collects minimal information necessary to operate the Service. When you authenticate via TikTok or other platforms, we receive an access token to publish content on your behalf. We do not collect personal data from viewers.

2. How We Use Information

Access tokens are used solely to publish video content to your social media accounts. We do not sell, share, or distribute your information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored securely on our private server with restricted file permissions. No data is stored in public or shared environments.

4. Third-Party Services

The Service integrates with YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook APIs. Your use of these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Retention

We retain authentication tokens only as long as necessary to provide the Service. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

6. Security

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, including encrypted storage and restricted access controls.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

9. Contact

For privacy concerns, visit storyforgesshorts.com.
